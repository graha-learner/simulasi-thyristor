Konsep ThyristorThyristor Concepts

Saklar elektronik yang mengunci sendiri setelah dipicu.An electronic switch that locks itself on once triggered.

Thyristor (SCR) adalah saklar tanpa bagian bergerak. Cukup “ditekan” sekali dengan sinyal kecil, ia langsung menyala dan tetap menyala selama arus utamanya masih mengalir. Komponen ini ada di pengatur kecepatan motor, pengisi aki, dan pengatur pemanas.A thyristor (SCR) is a switch with no moving parts. Give it one small nudge and it switches on, and stays on for as long as its main current keeps flowing. You will find it in motor speed controllers, battery chargers and heater controls.

Konsep utamaThe big idea

Tiga sifat yang membuat thyristor istimewaThree things that make a thyristor special

Hanya satu arahOne way only

Seperti pintu putar di stasiun, arus listrik hanya boleh lewat dari satu sisi (anoda) ke sisi lain (katoda). Arah sebaliknya ditolak, selama tegangannya masih di bawah batas kemampuan komponen.Like a turnstile, current may only pass from one side (anode) to the other (cathode). The opposite direction is blocked, as long as the voltage stays within the part’s rating.

Perlu dipicuNeeds a trigger

Walau arahnya sudah benar, thyristor tetap diam sampai kaki ketiganya, gate, menerima sinyal kecil. Sinyal itulah “tombol nyala”-nya.Even with the right direction, it stays off until its third leg, the gate, receives a small signal. That signal is its “on button”.

MengunciLatches

Setelah menyala, sinyal gate boleh dilepas dan thyristor tetap menyala. Gate tidak bisa mematikannya.Once on, the gate signal can be removed and it stays on. The gate cannot switch it off.

    Atur kapan thyristor dinyalakan, atur terang lampuChoose when it fires, choose the brightness

    Pada listrik AC, thyristor dipicu sekali di setiap gelombang. Semakin lambat dipicu (sudut α makin besar), semakin sedikit bagian gelombang yang sampai ke lampu, sehingga lampu makin redup. Inilah cara kerja peredup lampu dan pengatur kecepatan motor.On AC the thyristor is triggered once every wave. The later it fires (larger angle α), the less of the wave reaches the lamp, so the lamp gets dimmer. This is how dimmers and motor speed controllers work.

    Simulasi 3 · Tambah kapasitorSimulator 3 · Add a capacitor

    Kapasitor membuat lampu bisa diredupkan hampir sampai padamA capacitor lets the lamp dim almost to off

    Tanpa kapasitor, pemicu dari resistor hanya bisa menunda nyala thyristor sampai puncak gelombang (90°), jadi lampu paling redup masih mendapat separuh daya. Kapasitor bekerja seperti ember yang diisi lewat keran (potensiometer R): gate baru dipicu setelah ember cukup penuh. Makin kecil kerannya, makin lambat terisi, makin lambat thyristor menyala.Without a capacitor, a resistor trigger can only delay firing up to the wave’s peak (90°), so the dimmest setting still gets half the power. The capacitor acts like a bucket filled through a tap (potentiometer R): the gate fires only once the bucket is full enough. The smaller the tap, the slower it fills, the later the thyristor fires.

    Kapan menyala?When does it turn on?

    Tiga syarat menyalaThree conditions to turn on

    1

    Arah listrik benarCorrect direction

    Sisi anoda harus berpotensial lebih tinggi daripada sisi katoda. Kalau arahnya terbalik, thyristor menolak walaupun gate dipicu.The anode must be at a higher potential than the cathode. If the direction is reversed, it refuses even when the gate is triggered.

    2

    Gate mendapat sinyal pemicuThe gate gets a trigger

    Arus kecil masuk ke gate membuat thyristor menyala. Besarnya tergantung tipe: dari kurang dari 1 mA pada SCR gate peka sampai puluhan miliampere pada SCR daya (2N4444: paling besar 30 mA).A small current into the gate switches it on. How small depends on the type: under 1 mA for sensitive-gate SCRs, up to tens of milliamps for power SCRs (2N4444: at most 30 mA).

    3

    Arus utama cukup besarEnough main current

    Arus yang mengalir dari anoda ke katoda harus mencapai batas yang disebut arus kunci (latching current). Kalau tercapai, thyristor tetap menyala walaupun gate dilepas.The current flowing from anode to cathode must reach a level called the latching current. Once it does, the thyristor stays on even after the gate is released.

    Bagaimana mematikannya?How do you switch it off?

    Hentikan arus utamanyaStop the main current

    Melepas sinyal gate tidak mematikan thyristor yang sudah menyala. Satu-satunya cara adalah membuat arus utama, yaitu arus yang mengalir dari anoda ke katoda dan melewati lampu, turun hampir nol, di bawah batas yang disebut arus tahan (holding current).Releasing the gate does not switch off a thyristor that is already on. The only way is to make the main current, the current flowing from anode to cathode through the lamp, fall to almost zero, below a level called the holding current.

    DC

    Pada bateraiOn a battery

    Arus utama tidak pernah berhenti sendiri, jadi harus diputus, misalnya dengan membuka saklar.The main current never stops by itself, so it must be broken, for example by opening a switch.

    AC

    Pada listrik bolak-balikOn alternating current

    Arus utama turun ke nol di akhir setiap gelombang (50 kali per detik pada listrik 50 Hz), sehingga thyristor padam sendiri dan harus dipicu lagi di gelombang berikutnya.The main current falls to zero at the end of every wave (50 times a second on 50 Hz power), so the thyristor switches itself off and must be triggered again on the next wave.

Their main behaviour matched textbook theory; small numerical differences came from the instruments and the test circuits.
